    House rules

    Head Scratch Society is a friendly club for people who enjoy being stumped. We post short puzzles, illusions, memory tests and friendly debates. This is the clubhouse: a fresh scratcher every day, a few games, and the whole archive on the wall. Members welcome. The meeting is open.

    1. Every puzzle is fair.

      The clue is always in plain sight, even when the answer is sneaky. If you feel cheated, we got it wrong.

    2. Guess before you peek.

      Being stumped is the fun part. The answer will still be there in a minute.

    3. No made-up numbers.

      You will never read “most people fail this” here. We don't count who gets what wrong. Nothing you do on this site leaves your browser.

    4. Argue the answer, not the person.

      Disagreeing is the whole point. Being unkind is not.

    5. The arguing happens on the Page.

      Bring your answer to the comments on our Facebook Page. Answers get pinned once people have had a go.
